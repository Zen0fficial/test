Qlib adaptation notice

CompatibleTSTCN.predict in qlib_model_extensions_compat.py adapts
Microsoft Qlib v0.9.7 qlib/contrib/model/pytorch_tcn_ts.py.
Source: https://github.com/microsoft/qlib/blob/v0.9.7/qlib/contrib/model/pytorch_tcn_ts.py
Change: transpose the inference input's time/feature axes to match the native
train_epoch/test_epoch path. Native network, optimizer and losses are unchanged.

CompatibleInternalData._calc_perf in qlib_meta_parquet.py adapts the native
InternalData performance calculation in qlib/contrib/meta/data_selection/dataset.py.
Source: https://github.com/microsoft/qlib/blob/v0.9.7/qlib/contrib/meta/data_selection/dataset.py
Change: preserve datetime group labels explicitly under pandas 2; native Spearman
correlation, proxy training, masking and meta-learning remain unchanged.

HKStateAdapter.update in paper_qlib.py adapts the execution-state update in
Microsoft Qlib v0.9.7, qlib/rl/order_execution/strategy.py.
Source: https://github.com/microsoft/qlib/blob/v0.9.7/qlib/rl/order_execution/strategy.py
Changes: observed HK calendar indices/timestamps, accumulation of same-minute
fills, and explicit invalid-index/overfill/quote validation. Upstream metric
formulas and the native executors, exchange and accounting remain in use.

Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation.

workflows/manager.py and workflows/trainer.py adapt the TaskManager and
TrainerRM/DelayTrainerRM contracts from Qlib v0.9.7:
https://github.com/microsoft/qlib/blob/v0.9.7/qlib/workflow/task/manage.py
https://github.com/microsoft/qlib/blob/v0.9.7/qlib/model/trainer.py
Persistence uses local SQLite claims and immutable Parquet history instead of
MongoDB. Native Qlib task training callbacks and MLflow recorders remain in use.
Lease fencing, bounded retries and crash recovery are documented extensions.
